Privacy Policy

fullfill — Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 1st Dec 2025

Your privacy matters to us. fullfill is built to help you grow — not to collect unnecessary data or sell your information. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe.

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The fullfill mobile app
  • The fullfill website (fullfill.me)
  • Any features, content, or services we provide

By using fullfill, you agree to this policy.

2. What We Collect — and Why

We collect only the information needed to run the app, personalize your experience, and keep things secure.

A. Information You Provide

1. Account details

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)

Why: to create and manage your account.

2. Content you create

  • Journal entries
  • Reflections
  • Notes
  • Routine preferences
  • Saved activities
  • Check-ins

Why: to personalize your experience and let you revisit your progress. We never sell or publish this content.

B. Information Collected Automatically

1. Device & app data

  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Language
  • Timezone
  • Usage logs (e.g., feature usage, session duration)

Why: to fix bugs, improve performance, and understand feature usage.

2. Analytics

We use privacy-focused analytics to understand patterns without identifying individuals.

C. Payment Data

Payments are handled by Apple App Store and Google Play Store. We never store your full credit card data, only confirmation of payment status (successful, renewed, cancelled).

D. Optional Data

Enabling optional features like notifications or AI personalization requires your consent. You can turn these on or off anytime.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Run and maintain fullfill
  • Personalize your experience
  • Improve app features
  • Communicate important updates
  • Prevent misuse or fraud
  • Provide customer support
  • Conduct anonymized analytics

We do not use your data to target ads or sell it to anyone.

4. How We Protect Your Data

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS)
  • Secure cloud storage
  • Limited access controls
  • Routine security audits

Your journal and personal reflections remain private.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data only as long as needed to operate the service. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed permanently. Some anonymized analytics may remain but cannot identify you.

6. Sharing Your Data

We only share your data with trusted partners who help operate fullfill (cloud hosting, analytics tools, AI processing). These partners follow strict privacy and security standards. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

7. AI Features

  • AI personalization processes data securely
  • Your personal entries are not used to train public models
  • AI outputs are stored only as needed for features you use
  • You can opt out of AI features anytime

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location (EU, UK, US, etc.), you may have rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent for optional features
  • Object to certain types of processing

Email us anytime at contact@fullfill.me to request these.

9. Children

fullfill is for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. International Data Transfers

We may store data on servers outside your country and follow GDPR and other international standards to protect your information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Major changes will be communicated via the app or email.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, you can reach us at contact@fullfill.me. We read every message.
