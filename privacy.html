Privacy Policy

fullfill — Your Privacy, Your Peace of Mind
Effective Date: 13th Feb 2026

fullfill is here to help you grow — not to hoard data or sell your info. This page explains what we collect, why, and how we keep it safe.

1. Who This Applies To

  • The fullfill app
  • The fullfill website (fullfill.me)
  • All features, content, and services we provide

By using fullfill, you’re agreeing to this policy.

2. What We Collect (and Why)

We only gather what we need to make your experience awesome and secure.

A. Info You Give Us

  • Account details: name, username, email, encrypted password
  • Your content: journals, reflections, notes, routines, saved activities, check-ins

Why: To create/manage your account and personalize your journey. We never sell or share this.

B. Info We Collect Automatically

  • Device & app data: type, OS, app version, language, timezone, usage logs
  • Analytics: privacy-friendly tools for patterns — no individual tracking

Why: To improve fullfill, fix bugs, and understand feature usage.

C. Payment Info

All payments go through Apple or Google. We don’t store full card info — just confirmation of payment status.

D. Optional Info

Notifications, AI personalization, and other extras are optional. Turn them on or off anytime.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Run fullfill smoothly
  • Personalize your experience
  • Improve features
  • Keep your account safe
  • Communicate updates
  • Provide support
  • Do anonymized analytics

We never sell your data or target ads.

4. How We Protect Your Data

  • HTTPS encryption
  • Secure cloud storage
  • Strict access control
  • Regular security checks

Your journals and reflections stay private.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your info only as long as you use fullfill. Delete your account, and personal data is gone. Anonymized stats may remain but can’t identify you.

6. Sharing Your Data

We only share with trusted partners who help run fullfill — cloud hosts, analytics, AI services. They follow strict privacy rules. We never sell or share your data for ads.

7. AI Features

  • AI uses data securely to personalize your experience
  • Your entries are not used to train public AI models
  • Outputs stored only as long as needed
  • You can opt out anytime

8. Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct mistakes
  • Delete or export your data
  • Withdraw consent for optional features

Email hello@fullfill.me to exercise these rights.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions or privacy concerns, reach out at hello@fullfill.me. We read every message.
