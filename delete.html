Delete Your Account & Data

Updated 3 Dec 2025

This page explains how to permanently delete your Fullfill account and all associated data. Once deleted, your data cannot be restored.

What Will Be Deleted

  • Your Fullfill account
  • All journal entries
  • Progress data and routines
  • Preferences and activity history
  • Any data stored in your profile

Delete Your Account

To delete your account, open the Fullfill app and go to:
Settings → Account → Delete Account

Deletion happens instantly and is permanent.

If You Can’t Access the App

You can request deletion manually by contacting us using the link below. Your request must come from the email used to create your Fullfill account.

Request Account Deletion

How Long Deletion Takes

App deletion is instant. Email requests are processed within 7 days.

Data We May Retain

For security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance, limited non-personal technical logs may be retained for up to 30 days. These cannot identify you.
